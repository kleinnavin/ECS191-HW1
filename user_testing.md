# User Testing Write Up

### User 1

__5 Second Test__: 
They thought it was an app for tracking the stock market, but didn’t mention anything about being able to track one’s own financial investments.

__Where they got stuck/confused__: 
The input fields left them confused because they didn’t show any feedback or a way of continuing to the next field. 

__Answers to Debrief__:
They found the app easy to use and intuitive. They suggested being able to edit positions without having to delete it. I forgot to ask while recording, but the user described the app as a stock tracking tool for people with personal investments.

### User 2

__5 Second Test__: 
The user thought the app was used to analyze stocks but also didn’t mention anything about tracking a portfolio. 

__Where they got stuck/confused__: 
The user didn’t get stuck at any point. They completed both tasks without any difficulties but mentioned that it may be unclear what the purpose of the buttons are to first time users.

__Answers to Debrief__:
They rated the app a 2.33/5 on the difficulty scale for using the app. The user suggested that the buttons, particularly the portfolio insights button, be larger and have better descriptions. They described the app as “very well functioning”.

### User 3

__5 Second Test__: 
They thought that the app is used to track stocks that the user adds and also monitor details regarding your own positions.

__Where they got stuck/confused__: 
The user got stuck when there was no way to close the number pad. They exited out of the view and restarted the process of entering their position. Due to the confusion and restarting the process, they forgot to change the date on the position which led to inaccurate data. 

__Answers to Debrief__:
They found navigating the app and using the features intuitive. They mentioned that the button for adding stocks to the watchlist could be clearer and simpler. They described it as an app to track stocks and current positions.

### User 4

__5 Second Test__: 
They thought the app is used to track stocks.

__Where they got stuck/confused__: 
They didn’t have trouble adding the stock to their portfolio or using the portfolio insights button. They mentioned that the My Positions button was a little small which could create challenges for some users.

__Answers to Debrief__:
The user ranked the difficulty of using the app a 2/5. They liked the minimalist design of the app but thought the My Positions button could be improved because it looks similar to other stocks in the list. They described the app as something that allows you to log investment positions and track how they do over time.

### User 5

__*Important Note__: During the task phase, the user was not able to figure out how to get to the My Positions view which is a required step to test the portfolio insights (AI feature) function. In order to test this feature, I told them to navigate to the My Positions view.

__5 Second Test__: 
This user thought the app was used to look at stocks that they own.

__Where they got stuck/confused__: 
They didn’t realize that they could use the My Positions tab to input the stocks that they own. They were also confused by the distinction between all-time gain and daily gain. 

__Answers to Debrief__:
They gave the app a 3/5 for difficulty of use. They mentioned that navigating the app was difficult for someone who didn’t have background knowledge about finance. They suggested connecting the Watchlist and My Positions view in a more intuitive way. They described the app as a tool for people who want to keep up with both stocks that they own and don’t own. 

### Patterns and Points of Confusion
One thing multiple people struggled with was the process of entering information about a position. Several people found it confusing because the number pad would remain on the screen as the user goes through fields without having a way of closing it. It is clear that they were either expecting some sort of visual change or a way of clicking something to make the keyboard disappear. Another pattern that I noticed is that several testers felt the buttons needed improvement. Multiple users talked about the purpose of buttons being unclear. Also multiple people talked about how certain buttons were too small and not emphasized enough.

### Future Changes
I would definitely redesign the buttons to make them clearer (changing the color/size). I opted to simply use icons to convey what the buttons do, but I think after the user feedback labels would be a useful addition. I would also emphasize the more important buttons to help the user flow of the app. 

Although most users eventually figured it out, I think the input process could be more intuitive. Instead of having the user enter all of the information on the same screen, I would have them enter it sequentially in different steps. This method would give the user more feedback and not overwhelm them.

Another thing I would change is adding a feature that helps retain user interest. Users found the app to be very straightforward, which was one of my goals. However, I think a fun feature would be beneficial, especially to users who aren’t as interested in the stock market. I would add a “Stock of the Day” feature that shows some stock or other financial asset that is interesting or relevant to current events.
